## 02 · THE PROMPTING LAB
**Cover text:** `PROMPTING LAB` · **Level:** Beginner to Advanced

**Classroom description (paste into Skool):**
> The core of DGL x P2P. Learn RJ's prompting method and tested prompt structures, and the workflows that get premium results from affordable AI apps.

**Learning outcome:** Members can write structured prompts that give consistent, realistic, controllable results in any image or video app, and can debug a prompt that isn't working.

**Module 1: Prompting Foundations**
1.1. Why the prompt matters more than the app
1.2. The anatomy of a strong prompt: subject, scene, style, camera, light
1.3. Writing prompts in plain language vs structured formats
1.4. Negative prompts and what to leave out

*Assignment:* Rewrite one weak prompt three times using the anatomy framework and post all three results.

**Module 2: The DGL x P2P Method**
2.1. The DGL x P2P framework explained
2.2. Building reusable prompt templates
2.3. Prompt stacking and iteration loops
2.4. Debugging: why a prompt fails and how to fix it

*Assignment:* Build your first reusable template and test it on three different subjects.

**Module 3: Advanced Prompt Engineering**
3.1. Structured and code-style prompts (JSON and parameter formats)
3.2. Camera and lens language for realism
3.3. Lighting language for realism
3.4. Using AI chat assistants to write and refine prompts

*Assignment:* Create one structured (code-style) prompt and compare it side by side with a plain-language version.

**Resources:** Prompt anatomy cheat sheet; Camera and lens vocabulary list; Lighting vocabulary list; RJ's tested prompt templates [RJ TO ADD]
