## How entertainment leads to education

Drama AI and Relationship AI posts stay entertainment. They're never mixed with each other or turned into ads. Each one ends with a **bridge**:

1. **The story** (the hook and the cliffhanger)
2. **The reveal:** "Made 100% with AI."
3. **The bridge:** "I teach the exact workflow: characters, scenes, prompts."
4. **The CTA:** "Comment VAULT."

Then a related **AI Tutorial** post goes up 1–2 days later ("How I made the sister in Ep. 3 look the same in every scene"). That's how entertainment viewers become students.

## Rules

- Never post a "result" or "win" that isn't real and approved by the person in it.
- Drama AI and Relationship AI each get their own series and highlights. They're never combined.
- Every grid tile uses the black/white/chrome cover system. Replace any old purple or pink covers.
