# DGL BY RJ: Brand Blueprint

## 1. Core identity

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | DGL BY RJ |
| **Founder** | Rabyn Parks (RJ) |
| **Positioning** | SKILLS FOR CEOs WHO PROMPT TO PROFIT. |
| **Method** | DGL x P2P (Prompt to Profit) |
| **Community** | DGL BY RJ Vault, on Skool |
| **Website** | dglbyrj.com |
| **Audience** | Men and women: creators, entrepreneurs, business owners, beginners |
| **Objective** | Education, community growth and monetization |

### Mission
Give everyday creators and entrepreneurs the prompting skills, workflows and business systems to get premium results from affordable AI apps, and turn those skills into a brand and an income.

### The one-sentence pitch
> RJ teaches you tested prompting techniques and workflows that get premium results from affordable AI apps, and how to turn that skill into content, clients and products.

### Why DGL is different
1. **Tested, not theoretical.** The prompts and workflows are tested in real content first.
2. **Affordable over expensive.** It's about getting better results from the apps you can afford, not buying the priciest tool.
3. **Skill to business.** Members don't just learn to make AI content. They learn to build a brand and monetize it like a CEO.
4. **App-agnostic.** The method still works when apps and models change.

## 2. Messaging

### Brand pillars (what every message reinforces)
1. **Prompting is the skill.** The prompt matters more than the app.
2. **Create like a studio.** Realistic photos, cinematic video, consistent characters, full series.
3. **Grow like a brand.** Content pillars, series, hooks and systems.
4. **Profit like a CEO.** Products, services, clients and community.

### Tagline options
- Skills For CEOs Who Prompt To Profit. *(primary)*
- Prompt Like A CEO.
- Tested Prompts. Real Results. Affordable Apps.
- From Prompt To Profit.

### Words we use / words we avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| CEO, skill, system, workflow, tested, studio, build, Vault | "get rich", "easy money", "passive income overnight" |
| "learn the skill", "build the business" | any income figure that isn't verified |
| premium, clean, precise | "hack", "cheat code" (except for real, specific techniques) |

### Voice
Confident CEO energy: sharp, direct and premium, but generous with real knowledge. Short, punchy lines. A mentor who has tested it and is bringing you in. No hype and no income promises.

## 3. Visual identity

**Rule: black, white and chrome silver only. Never purple or hot pink.**

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Onyx black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Surface | Graphite | `#1C1C1E` |
| Text and contrast | Pure white | `#FFFFFF` |
| Accent | Chrome silver | `#C0C0C0` |
| Chrome highlight | Bright chrome | `#E8E8EA` |
| Chrome shadow | Steel | `#8A8D91` |

**Chrome effect:** a vertical gradient `#E8E8EA → #C0C0C0 → #8A8D91 → #E8E8EA` on display type and logo marks. Use it on headlines and the logo only, never on body text.

**Typography (suggested):**
- Headlines: a wide, geometric sans in all caps (for example *Syncopate*, *Michroma* or *Montserrat ExtraBold*) with wide letter spacing
- Body: a clean sans (*Inter* or *Montserrat Regular*)

**Look and feel:** luxury technology and a modern CEO office. Dark backgrounds, crisp white type, chrome accents, lots of negative space, cinematic lighting and glossy reflections.

### Applying it everywhere

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Instagram Highlight covers | Black circle, white or chrome all-caps label (DRAMA, LOVE, HOW TO, WINS, P2P, CEO) |
| Reel covers | Black or dark-cinematic frame, chrome headline at the top third, pillar label at the bottom |
| Skool classroom covers | Black background, number (`01`) in chrome, classroom name in white caps, thin chrome line |
| Skool banner | "DGL BY RJ VAULT" in chrome, "Skills For CEOs Who Prompt To Profit" in white below |
| Website and products | Same palette and type. Chrome buttons with black text, or black buttons with a chrome border |

### AI prompt for on-brand graphics
> Minimal luxury technology graphic, matte black background, polished chrome silver 3D lettering reading "[TEXT]", crisp white secondary text, soft studio reflections, high contrast, premium CEO aesthetic, clean negative space, no purple, no pink.
