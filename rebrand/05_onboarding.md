# Student Onboarding System

**Goal:** every new member gets a first win within 7 days, because members who finish something early tend to stay.

## 1. Before they join (Skool membership questions)
1. What do you want to create with AI? (images, video, stories, business)
2. What's your experience level: brand new, some experience, or already earning?
3. Where did you find DGL BY RJ?

The answers decide which path RJ recommends in the welcome DM, and they show which content brings in members.

## 2. Welcome DM (on approval)
```
Welcome to the DGL BY RJ Vault, [name]! 🖤
Skills for CEOs who prompt to profit.

Your first 3 steps:
1. Open 01 · START HERE and watch the welcome
2. Post your intro in the community (name + what you want to master)
3. Pick your path: Beginner, Creator, or Business

I'll see you in the Showcase. — RJ
```

## 3. First 7 days

| Day | Task | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Watch the welcome, read the rules, post an introduction | 01 · Module 1 |
| 2 | Set up the toolkit and prompt folder | 01 · Module 2 |
| 3 | Choose a path | 01 · Module 3 |
| 4 | Prompting Foundations: lesson 1 and 2 | 02 · Module 1 |
| 5 | Rewrite a weak prompt three times | 02 · Module 1 assignment |
| 6 | Post the result in the Showcase for feedback | 13 |
| 7 | Give feedback to 2 other members | 13 |

## 4. Paths after onboarding

- **Beginner (New CEO):** 02 Prompting Lab → 05 App Vault → 03 Photo Studio → 04 Video Production
- **Creator (Storyteller):** 06 Character University → 07 Drama AI Studios or 08 Relationship AI Studios → 09 Faceless Systems
- **Business (CEO):** 10 Social Media CEO School → 11 Digital Product Empire → 12 Client Services & DFY

## 5. Levels and unlocks (suggested)

Skool gives members points when other members like their posts and comments, and levels come from those points. Courses can unlock at a level.

| Level | Unlocks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01, 02, 05, 13, 14 | Foundations open to everyone |
| 2 | 03, 04 | Encourages posting the first assignments |
| 3 | 06, 07, 08, 09 | Creator classrooms after the basics |
| 4 | 10, 11, 12 | Business classrooms for engaged members |

*Optional: unlock everything for paid tiers and use levels only as recognition. RJ decides based on the current pricing.*

## 6. Retention touchpoints

- **Day 7:** check-in post or DM: "What was your first win?"
- **Day 14:** invite them to the weekly challenge
- **Day 30:** feature their best work (with permission) and ask what they want to learn next
- **Inactive for 14 days:** "Hey [name], we just added [new lesson]. Here's a 5-minute one to get back in."
