# Rebrand Marketing Strategy

**Goal:** turn viewers into DGL BY RJ Vault members without income promises, by showing the skill and inviting people to learn it.

**The funnel:**
Entertainment Reel (reach) → Tutorial or education post (trust) → "Comment VAULT" (lead) → DM (conversation) → Vault page (member) → Start Here (first win) → Showcase (proof that feeds new content)

---

## 1. Rebrand announcement

> **[0–2s, chrome text on black]** "I'm changing everything."
> **[2–8s]** "For months you've watched my AI drama and relationship stories. What you didn't see is the system behind them."
> **[8–16s, quick cuts of the best AI content]** "Tested prompts. Realistic characters. Full series, made with affordable AI apps."
> **[16–24s]** "This is DGL BY RJ. Skills for CEOs who prompt to profit."
> **[24–30s]** "The Vault is open. Comment VAULT and I'll send you in."

## 3. Reel hooks bank

**Drama AI:** "She didn't know the camera was AI too." · "Episode 1 of the most dramatic AI reality show." · "Wait for what the sister says."
**Relationship AI:** "When he remembers the little things…" · "Red flag or green flag? Comment below." · "This couple doesn't exist."
**Tutorials:** "Same app, same price, completely different result. Here's the prompt difference." · "Stop paying for expensive AI apps until you learn this." · "How I keep my AI character's face the same in every scene."
**Education and business:** "The prompt matters more than the app." · "Prompting is a business skill. Here's why." · "3 ways creators turn AI skills into income (no hype)."

## 4. DM conversion scripts

**When someone comments VAULT:**
```
Hey [name]! 🖤 Thanks for commenting.
Quick question so I send you the right thing:
are you here more for (1) creating AI content, or (2) turning it into a business?
```
**If they answer (1):**
```
Perfect. Inside the Vault, start with the Prompting Lab, then AI Photo Studio and AI Video Production.
You'll learn the tested prompts and workflows behind my videos, step by step.
Here's the link: [VAULT LINK]
```
**If they answer (2):**
```
Love that. Inside the Vault, the Business path covers Social Media CEO School, Digital Product Empire and Client Services.
You learn the skill, then how to package and sell it.
Here's the link: [VAULT LINK]
```
**Follow-up after 48 hours with no reply:**
```
Hey [name], just checking you got the link 🖤 Any questions about what's inside? Happy to help.
```
*Send these by hand or through an approved automation tool. Don't send unsolicited bulk DMs.*

## 6. Student engagement campaigns (monthly rhythm)

| Week | Campaign |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Prompt Challenge:** RJ posts one image and members try to recreate it |
| 2 | **Series Week:** members post Ep. 1 of a Drama AI or Relationship AI series |
| 3 | **Business Week:** members post an offer or product draft for feedback |
| 4 | **Showcase Friday:** RJ features the best work (with permission) and posts it to Instagram as a VAULT WIN |

## 8. Guardrails

- No income promises or unverified figures. Show the skill, not a fantasy.
- Results only with real proof and the member's permission.
- Every claim about the Vault has to be true when it's posted. Don't advertise a classroom before it exists.
